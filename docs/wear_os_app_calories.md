# The Wear OS App: calories

> Calories on the Android wrist, in two parts: the wrist's own logic in Dart (plan 305) and the native source that feeds it (plan 306). A separate page from [The Wear OS App](wear_os_app.md) to keep that page under the indexing band. The Apple wrist's version is plans 134c2, 134e and 135; the phone's half (plans 134c1 and 135) needs no change.

## What the wrist does with a calorie count

The platform workout's active energy (kilocalories, cumulative since the workout began) reaches the wrist through an optional interface, `WatchEnergySource` (`lib/watch/sensors/watch_sensor_recording.dart`: `activeEnergyPermission()` and `activeEnergy()`), kept apart from `WatchSensorSource` so every existing source and test double stays as it was. When the source also implements it, `WatchSensorRecorder.start` asks the permission and, on `granted`, listens for every session whatever its modality. A refused permission, a source without energy, or no reading changes nothing.

Each reading does three things, in this order:

1. **Stored** as an `energy` sensor sample stamped with the instant it arrived (the same append-only store as heart rate).
2. **Announced** to the phone as a live `energy_live` frame (`WatchSessionEngine.announceEnergy`): the running total, live only, never stored and never queued (a frame the radio refuses is dropped and the next reading replaces it). The total is the restart-aware chain over the stored counts (`WatchSensorSummaries.activeEnergy`, `lib/watch/session/watch_sensor_summaries.dart`): a count smaller than the one before means the counter restarted, as after a recovered workout, so it adds whole and the total never falls. A frame goes out the first time for a session, when the total fell (only possible with no stored count to chain), or when it rose by at least half a kilocalorie and at least five seconds passed since the last frame. A non-finite or negative value sends nothing; so does a session that is not active. A frame the protocol refuses leaves no mark, so the next reading may go.
3. **Shown** on the rest screen: `N kcal burned` as one small line under the rest clock, once at least half a kilocalorie is measured (`WatchSensorLabels.activeEnergy`, `WatchLoggingState.activeEnergyLabel`). It scales down rather than overflowing and is spoken as `Calories burned`, `N kilocalories burned`. The logging screen and the Workout done screen show no calories (the Apple wrist shows them on rest only).

The session's total also rides the `session_end` entry as `activeEnergyKcal` (wrist-created and followed ends alike), absent when nothing was measured; a zero total is never sent.

The wrist's diagnostics log coalesces live `energy_live` sends to one `frame_sent` line a minute (`WearWatchTransport.chattyTypes`), as the Apple bridge does, so a reading every few seconds does not push everything else out of the log.

## The contract between the two halves

`WearSensorSource` (`lib/watch/sensors/wear_sensor_source.dart`) implements `WatchEnergySource`: method `activeEnergyPermission` on `omnitrain/wear_sensors/methods` (`granted`, `denied` or `unavailable`, asking the user first when undetermined) and `EventChannel('omnitrain/wear_sensors/energy')` of numbers, cumulative kilocalories. A build without the native half fails every call with a missing-plugin error and reads `unavailable`, so the wrist behaves as it did before calories. A refusal is remembered for the life of the process (the workout asks before it starts and the recorder asks again, and Android prompts a second time after a first "Don't allow").

## The native half (plan 306)

The numbers come from the platform workout plan 300 already runs: Health Services' `ExerciseClient`. `WearSensors` answers `activeEnergyPermission`: `unavailable` unless the exercise client lists `CALORIES_TOTAL` for the generic workout type, `granted` when the physical-activity permission (`ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION`, declared in the wear manifest) is held, otherwise one system prompt ("Allow OmniTrain to access your physical activity?") whose answer is `granted` or `denied`. `WearPlatformWorkoutStore.begin` asks that question right after the heart-rate one and before it starts the exercise, because the exercise's data types are fixed at its start and the shell starts the workout before the recorder asks. `WearWorkout` then reads the exercise client's capabilities, adds `CALORIES_TOTAL` to the heart-rate data type only when the permission is held and the exercise type supports it (any failure is a workout without calories, as before), registers an update callback before starting the exercise (cleared when the start fails and when the workout ends), and each update's cumulative `CALORIES_TOTAL` goes to `EnergyStream` (`omnitrain/wear_sensors/energy`), which hands it to the Dart recorder. With the permission refused, or calories unsupported, the workout and the heart rate run as before and the wrist shows no calories.

**The number includes the resting burn.** Health Services' `CALORIES_TOTAL` is the total calories burned since the exercise began, "including basal rate and activity"; `ExerciseClient` has no active-only figure. So the wrist's `kcal burned` and the `activeEnergyKcal` it sends on `session_end` (a field the protocol describes as the active energy above resting) are the platform's total and read higher than the same workout on an Apple wrist, by roughly a kilocalorie a minute for an adult. Subtracting an estimated resting burn would present a guess as a measurement, so it is not done; whether to is a product decision.

## Verified by

`test/wear_calories_native_test.dart` (plan 306): the store's asking order and the remembered refusal, and source guards on the Kotlin (the permission answer, the energy stream, the workout's data types, callback and clearing, the registrar and the manifest; there is no Kotlin test harness). On the emulator (Wear OS 5, Health Services synthetic walking data) the prompt appeared after the heart-rate one, an allow gave `granted` and a cumulative number that grew (3.3 then 6.9 kcal in 24 readings), and a refusal gave `denied` with no number and the heart rate unaffected.

`test/wear_calories_test.dart` (plan 305): the summaries chain (restart, window ends, base count, filters, rounding), the recorder (store, instants, denial, no energy source, stop, every modality), the announce rule (first, half a kilocalorie, five seconds, equal, fall, invalid, ended, new session), a recovered counter, the frame against the shared fixture and the validator, a refused frame leaving no mark, the session end (wrist-created, followed, absent, restart, conformant), the label, the rest screen (line, spoken form, 192 and 227 dp), the Wear source's channels and the transport's log coalescing. Not seen on a real watch: owner check 22 in [The Wear OS App](wear_os_app.md) "Checks on a watch".
