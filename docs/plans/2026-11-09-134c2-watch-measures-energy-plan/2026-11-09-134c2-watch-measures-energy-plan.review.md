## Plan 134c2 — the wrist measures energy and sends it on the end

### Q1: the chain in `activeEnergy`, and the window on a followed end
Severity: **none**.
`WatchSensorSummaries.activeEnergy` (`WatchSensorSummaries.swift:161-200`) is the steps chain verbatim (compare `:126-157`): filter to kind + finite + `>= 0`, sort by `(wholeMilliseconds(recordedAt), sequence)`, seed `previous` from the last count before the window, then `total += sample.value >= previous ? sample.value - previous : sample.value`. The brief's three sequences:
- *restart in the middle*: the smaller value is treated as the energy since the restart and the predecessor's banked amount is kept — asserted by `WatchActiveEnergyTests.testS13471ASmallerValueIsARestartAndIsItselfTheEnergySinceIt` (100 → 40 = 140.0).
- *two samples with the same millisecond*: both survive the same-ms sort (by `sequence`) and the chain adds them in order; a same-ms pair with the higher-sequence sample carrying a *smaller* value would bank it as a restart — the only way to produce that at the same millisecond is a counter restart inside one millisecond, which the platform cannot emit, and the semantics are identical to the reviewed steps branch (the shared chain's known edge, not a new one).
- *a sample after the window end*: excluded by the `inside` filter (`at >= first && at <= last`, `WatchSensorSummaries.swift:189-192`); `WatchSensorSummaryTests` S-234 asserts the same boundary semantics for heart rate. Non-finite values cannot reach the window comparisons: filtered earlier by `isFinite`.
Because the chain computes a range *difference*, clock skew on a followed session cannot produce an over-count: a window that begins before the counter's first sample starts the chain at 0 and counts everything (correct); a window that begins after samples seeds `previous` from that sample and counts only the growth after it (correct). The tail (phone `endedAt` behind wrist-recorded samples) can low-ball — the established rule heart rate follows (`WatchSensorSummaryTests` S-234: the 200 bpm at 10:22 after the phone's `at` 10:20 is dropped).
`sessionEndEvent` passes `WatchSensorSummaries.wireInstant(origin.startedAt)` and `wireInstant(endedAt)` (`WatchSessionEngine.swift:1695-1696`); for a followed session `origin.startedAt` is the phone's (`WatchSessionEngine.swift:600-603`: `startedAt: existing?.startedAt ?? startedAtOf(entryMaps, fallback: sentAt)`) — the window is the session's window, which is what D-13473 says. Guarded by `testS13475AFollowedSessionCarriesBothItsMarkAndItsEnergyTotal`.

### Q2: can the end be built twice with different energy?
Severity: **none**. `captureSessionEnd` (`WatchSessionEngine.swift:1667-1690`) refuses when `knowsObservation(WatchSessionCapture.sessionEndId(sessionId))`, and `knowsObservation` (`:1798-1800`) holds both still-stored and acknowledged ids (`acknowledgedObservationIds.formUnion` on receipt, `:2062`; drained from confirmations on restore, `:157`). So a session reopened and ended again, or ended by two messages (lifecycle `:717`, snapshot `:599`, the wrist's own transition `:1292`), builds exactly one end — the doc comment's claim ("no end is ever re-emitted with different values") is true for the energy field like every field. A late energy sample after the end can only change the *store*, never the already-built observation; the S-13474 "no sample → no key" arm and the S-13473/S-13475 arms are covered by tests that would fail without D-13473.
