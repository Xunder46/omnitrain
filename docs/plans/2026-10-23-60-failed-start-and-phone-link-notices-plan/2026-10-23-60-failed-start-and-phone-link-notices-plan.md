## Goal and acceptance

Owner decision (chat, 2026-10-23): tell the user only about phone-link problems they can act on; background sends that fail keep queueing quietly, and the existing "N entries waiting for your phone" line stays.

| What you do | Now | After 60 |
|---|---|---|
| Tap a routine whose copy has just gone from the watch (a Sync replaced the list between the screen drawing and your tap) | nothing happens | a small grey line on the Start screen: "That routine is no longer on the watch. Tap Sync."; the line goes away when the surface next refreshes (a Sync answer, a phone arrival) or you start another routine |
| Tap Sync and the phone never answers (the spinner runs 20 s, then stops) | the spinner just disappears, nothing says it failed | a grey line under the phone status: "No answer from your phone. Open OmniTrain on it and try again."; it goes away when you tap Sync again or when any answer from the phone arrives |
| The watch cannot bring its link to the phone up at all (WatchConnectivity unsupported, or activation ended with an error) | no sync ever works and nothing says why | a grey line on the Start screen: "Phone link unavailable. Restart the watch app."; it goes away when the link activates |
| A background send fails (phone out of range, app asleep) | queued and retried; after 90 s "N entries waiting for your phone" | unchanged |
| Start a routine that is already running / a normal start | unchanged | unchanged (no message when you land in the running workout) |

Not in this plan: showing every failed send (owner chose not to); a retry button; the Free-workout picker's vanished-row case (the row simply does nothing; left as is); the Dart twin.
